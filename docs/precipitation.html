Rain and Surface Moisture

Visibility Reduction: Heavy downpours impair visual reference during visual approach phases and force aircraft to rely strictly on instrument landing systems (ILS).


Braking & Hydroplaning: Wet runways reduce tire traction, necessitating larger safety buffers between successive landings and takeoffs, which slows down the airport arrival/departure rate.

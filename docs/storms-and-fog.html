Operational Impact Breakdown

Thunderstorms: Severe turbulence, lightning, and strong downdrafts force aircraft to reroute around storm cells, causing en-route delays and increased fuel consumption. Ground crews must stop refuel and baggage handling during severe lightning strikes for safety.


Dense Fog: Low visibility requires airports to initiate Low Visibility Procedures (LVP). This increases aircraft spacing during taxiing, takeoff, and landing, drastically reducing runway capacity and causing cascade delays across network operations.

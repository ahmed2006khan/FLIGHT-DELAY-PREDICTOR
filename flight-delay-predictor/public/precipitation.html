Rain and wet surfaces

Visibility. Heavy downpours make visual approaches harder, so aircraft rely on instrument landing systems.

Braking and hydroplaning. Wet runways reduce tyre grip, so larger gaps are kept between landings and take-offs. That slows the number of flights the airport can handle.

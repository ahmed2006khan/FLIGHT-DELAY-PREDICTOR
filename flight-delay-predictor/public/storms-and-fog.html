Operational impact

Thunderstorms. Severe turbulence, lightning and strong downdrafts force aircraft to reroute around storm cells, causing en-route delays and extra fuel use. Ground crews must pause refuelling and baggage handling during lightning for safety.

Dense fog. Low visibility starts Low Visibility Procedures, which increase spacing during taxi, take-off and landing. Runway capacity drops and delays cascade across the network.

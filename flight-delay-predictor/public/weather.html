How weather affects flights

Pick a topic to see how it changes the delay score.

⛈️

Storms and fog

Thunderstorms and dense fog cause ground holds and wider spacing between aircraft, adding significant delays.
💨

Wind speed

Sustained or crosswinds above 30–50 km/h change approach paths and slow runway clearance.
🌧️

Rain

Rain reduces visibility and braking margins, so air traffic control lowers landing and departure rates.

Connection risk

Estimate the chance of missing your onward flight given a predicted arrival delay and the connection window.

Your connection

All times in local airport time.

Typical domestic MCT is 45 min; international often 60–90.

How it works

We add your expected arrival delay to the scheduled arrival, then compare the remaining buffer to the minimum connection time (adjusted for terminal changes). If the buffer is tight, risk rises quickly — airlines usually will not hold the second flight.

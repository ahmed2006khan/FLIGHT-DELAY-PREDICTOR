Risk levels

Every prediction is placed into one of three levels, so you can decide quickly.

Risk scale

0%35%65% – 100%
Low risk

Under 35%

Operations are running smoothly with little weather or traffic disruption. Arrive at the usual time.

Medium risk

35% to 65%

Moderate congestion or unfavourable weather is likely. Watch for updates and allow extra time.

High risk

Over 65%

Severe weather or heavy congestion is likely to affect departures. Check with the airline and keep plans flexible.

Wind factors

Crosswinds. Strong sideways wind near the runway makes landings harder and can trigger go-arounds or diversions to other airports.

Tailwinds and headwinds. A tailwind on landing needs a longer runway to slow down. Strong headwinds en route add flight time and fuel burn.

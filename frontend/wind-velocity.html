Wind Factors Explained

Crosswinds: High lateral wind components near runways challenge landing stability, occasionally triggering go-arounds and diversions to alternate airports.


Tailwinds & Jet Streams: Tailwinds during landing require longer runway distances to decelerate. Conversely, strong en-route headwinds significantly increase flight duration and fuel burn.

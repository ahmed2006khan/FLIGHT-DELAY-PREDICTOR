Risk Assessment Framework

Standardized risk classifications for delayed departure evaluations.

LOW RISK

Delay Probability < 35%. Operations are running smoothly with minimal operational or weather disruptions.

MEDIUM RISK

Delay Probability 35% – 65%. Moderate traffic congestion or unfavorable local weather expected.

HIGH RISK

Delay Probability > 65%. Severe weather warnings or heavy congestion are severely impacting arrival/departure schedules.
